Delete your Curated account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Curated and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE, and tap Delete my account.

Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone. You're signed out when it's done.

By email

If you can't use the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL — TO BE ADDED] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". Include your username if you remember it.

To protect your account, we only act on requests from the email address on the account. We'll confirm by email when your account has been deleted.

What's deleted

  • Your profile, username and profile photo
  • Your trips — live, finished and drafts — with their days, places and photos
  • Your comments, likes and saved places
  • Your plans, and your membership of plans others shared with you
  • Your follows, notifications, and trips sent to or by you
  • People you blocked, and reports you filed

What's kept

  • Places other people saved from your trips stay in their own lists, without your name or photos.
  • Reports other people made about your content are kept for safety review, marked as deleted.
